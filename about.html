Our Story

Global GPA was created by an engineering student at a Pune-based university who kept running into the same problem: needing to convert CGPA to percentage for job applications, and finding that every online tool either used the generic CBSE formula (wrong for SPPU students) or was buried under pop-up ads.

The solution was straightforward — build a single, clean, fast tool that supports every major Indian university formula, works offline, and never asks for your email address. That tool is this website.

What We Build

Global GPA is a static, single-file web tool — no backend, no database, no server calls. Every calculation happens entirely in your browser. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device.
  • The page loads in under a second on any connection.
  • It works offline once loaded — ideal for college Wi-Fi conditions.
  • There are no accounts, no cookies, and no tracking of any kind.

Our Values

🎯 Accuracy First

We research each university's official grading ordinance before adding a formula. If we're unsure, we say so. Every formula on the site cites the institution it comes from.

🔒 Zero Data Collection

We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party scripts. There are no cookies. Nothing about your visit is recorded anywhere.

⚡ Performance

The entire site is a single HTML file with no external dependencies. No CDN, no framework, no font imports. It loads fast on a 2G connection.

♿ Accessibility

The tool works on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop — and respects your system's dark mode preference. No app install required.

Supported Universities

We currently support conversion formulas for: SPPU (Pune University), VTU, Mumbai University, Anna University, AKTU/UPTU, Delhi University, GTU, KTU (Kerala), RGPV, JNTU, Osmania University, Bangalore University, IITs, and NITs.

If your university isn't listed or uses a different formula, contact us with the official formula source and we'll add it.

Disclaimer

Global GPA is an independent tool and is not affiliated with any university, examination board, or government body. All conversion results are approximate. For official documentation — job applications, visa applications, university admissions — always obtain a formal percentage equivalence certificate from your university's registrar or examination section.
