2. Description of Service

Global GPA is a free, browser-based academic calculator that provides approximate conversions between academic grading formats used in India and internationally, including:

  • CGPA to Percentage (and reverse)
  • CGPA to GPA on a 4.0 scale (and reverse)
  • SGPA to CGPA calculation
  • Marks to CGPA/SGPA estimation
  • University-specific conversion formulas (SPPU, VTU, Mumbai University, Anna University, and others)

The service is provided free of charge, without registration, and with no obligation or commercial relationship between the user and Global GPA.
