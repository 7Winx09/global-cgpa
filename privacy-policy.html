Privacy Policy

Global GPA is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. Short version: we collect nothing.

Last updated: June 2025  ·  Effective immediately

Plain-language summary: Global GPA runs entirely in your browser. We have no servers processing your data, no cookies tracking you, no analytics watching your behaviour, and no accounts requiring your email. Every calculation you perform stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

1. Who We Are

Global GPA (globalgpa.in) is a free academic utility tool that helps Indian university students convert CGPA to percentage, SGPA to CGPA, and perform related grade calculations. The website is operated as an independent, non-commercial student project.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Your CGPA, grades, or academic results entered into the calculator
  • Your IP address or device identifiers
  • Your location or geographic data
  • Browser fingerprints or session data
  • Any form of behavioural or usage analytics

All calculations are performed locally in your browser using JavaScript. No data is transmitted to any server at any point.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Global GPA does not use cookies of any kind — not session cookies, tracking cookies, or persistent cookies.

We use one item of browser localStorage: a single key (gpa-theme) that stores your dark/light mode preference so it persists across visits. This value never leaves your browser. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.

4. Third-Party Services

Global GPA loads no third-party scripts, fonts, stylesheets, or resources. The entire website — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — is contained in a single self-hosted file. There are:

  • No Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager
  • No Facebook Pixel or Meta SDK
  • No advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • No CDN-hosted libraries (jQuery, Bootstrap, etc.)
  • No social media widgets or share buttons that phone home

Because no external resources are loaded, no third party can track you via this website.

5. Children's Privacy

Global GPA is a general-purpose academic tool appropriate for all ages, including students under 18. Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, there is no risk of children's data being stored or shared. We comply fully with the intent of COPPA (US) and equivalent Indian regulations regarding minors' data.

6. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is no stored data to access, correct, export, or delete. However, if you believe we have inadvertently collected any information about you, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether any data exists
  • Request immediate deletion of any such data
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at our contact page.

7. Indian IT Act Compliance

Global GPA operates in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011. Since we do not collect, store, or process any Sensitive Personal Data or Information (SPDI) as defined under these rules, the mandatory disclosure and consent provisions do not apply — but we disclose this policy voluntarily in the spirit of transparency.

8. Changes to This Policy

If our privacy practices ever change — for example, if we add hosting analytics — we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of Global GPA after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Reach us via our Contact page. We typically respond within 48 hours.
